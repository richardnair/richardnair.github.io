### Applications for Research Positions
Feel free to discuss positions with me before making a formal application by emailing me or apply directly. Research positions will usually be advertised at least both here, and on our university web pages.

### Prospective Fellowships and Studentships
There are a variety of funding schemes available to support researchers at PhD and postdoctoral level available in Ireland and internationally.
Please get in contact if you are interested in opportunities to apply with the PSB group.
No need to send a full proposal at this stage but please do indicate why you are specifically interested in making an application to work with us and we can take it from there if suitable. 
Please note we will ignore generic/mass emailed/obvious LLM-slop enquiries.
- [Research Ireland PhD Funding](https://www.researchireland.ie/funding/government-ireland-postgraduate/)
- [Research Ireland Postdoctoral Funding](https://research.ie/funding-category/postdoctoral/)
- [Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions](https://marie-sklodowska-curie-actions.ec.europa.eu/actions/postdoctoral-fellowships)
- other funding schemes also exist  


### Master and Capstone projects, desk studies and internships for TCD students
If you are a student at TCD and are interested in doing a project in the PSB group, please drop me an email. 
I submit project titles when a call goes out but I also am happy to support interesting ideas in the general areas of plant biology (particularly belowground!), sustainability and data/technology applications to both.  
We also often host paid internships in the group over the summer. These depend on needs and available funds, and we have hosted both biologists and engineers. Typically these are advertised to TCD students, but rare exceptions can be made for a particularly good fit.
